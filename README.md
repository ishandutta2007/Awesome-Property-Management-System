# Awesome-Property-Management-System

## Top Property Management System (PMS) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Residential & Multifamily Property Management, Leasing, Accounting, Maintenance & Resident Portals*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Property Management Systems (PMS)**. These systems help property managers and owners handle units, leases, rent collection, maintenance, accounting, and resident communications for residential, multifamily, and related portfolios.



**Examples** include Yardi Voyager, AppFolio, Buildium, Entrata, DoorLoop, Hemlane, Rent Manager, Innago, Propertyware, and ResMan (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured residential and multifamily PMS platforms are largely commercial. Stronger open options exist for hospitality and short-term rentals (**Kamra PMS**, related projects) and via ERP modules (**Odoo PMS**). This section lists the best available open resources and is realistic about the residential PMS gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Yardi Voyager](https://www.yardi.com/)**  

  Enterprise property management platform widely used for large multifamily and commercial portfolios, with deep accounting and operational capabilities.



- **[AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/)**  

  Cloud-based property management software popular with mid-market residential operators, offering leasing, accounting, maintenance, and AI-assisted features.



- **[Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/)**  

  Property management platform (part of RealPage) focused on residential and HOA management with strong tools for smaller to mid-sized portfolios.



- **[Entrata](https://www.entrata.com/)**  

  Multifamily property management platform providing leasing, resident experience, and operational tools for larger communities.



- **[DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/)**  

  Modern property management software for residential landlords and managers covering marketing, leasing, accounting, and maintenance.



- **[Hemlane](https://www.hemlane.com/)**  

  Property management platform aimed at independent landlords and small managers with leasing and operations features.



- **[Rent Manager](https://www.rentmanager.com/)**  

  Flexible property management software used across residential and commercial portfolios with strong customization options.



- **[Innago](https://www.innago.com/)**  

  Free/affordable property management tools focused on landlords and small portfolios for rent collection and basic operations.



- **[Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/)**  

  Property management solution (part of RealPage) oriented toward residential and single-family portfolios.



- **[ResMan](https://www.resman.com/)**  

  Multifamily property management platform offering operations, leasing, and resident engagement tools.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Kamra PMS](https://github.com/Kamra-PMS/kamra-pms)**  

  Open-source property management system for hotels and short-term rentals built on Frappe. Includes front desk, booking engine, folios, housekeeping, POS, and related hospitality features.



- **[Odoo PMS / OCA PMS modules](https://github.com/OCA/pms)**  

  Open-source property management modules on the Odoo platform supporting multi-property management and related real-estate operations.



- **[Pesan PMS and hospitality open projects](https://github.com/pesanio/pesan-pms)**  

  Open-source PMS efforts focused on hotels, homestays, villas, and accommodation management (bookings, guests, rooms, staff).

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Kamra PMS** or similar open hospitality systems for hotels and short-term rentals.

- Leveraging **Odoo PMS modules** when already running Odoo or needing multi-property capabilities.

- Building lightweight custom solutions for very small portfolios with open ERP modules and portals.

- Accepting that full residential/multifamily PMS features (complex accounting, resident screening, compliance, large-scale operations, and integrations) still favor commercial platforms (Yardi, AppFolio, Buildium, Entrata, Rent Manager, etc.).

- Combining open tools for specific functions (maintenance, portals) with a commercial core PMS.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: For hospitality/short-term — deploy Kamra or an Odoo-based PMS → configure units, rates, and bookings → add housekeeping and billing. For residential — start with open ERP property modules → layer portals and maintenance tracking → integrate payments. Suitable mainly for smaller or specialized operators. Most professional residential and multifamily managers continue to rely on established commercial PMS platforms.
